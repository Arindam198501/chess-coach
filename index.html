Camera

Press “Start live camera”, or take a photo of the board.
👁 camera · tap
  1. Put the phone/webcam directly above the board, with the whole board in view.
  2. Tap the 4 outer corners of the board in order: a1 → h1 → h8 → a8 (a1 = White’s bottom-left). Drag a dot to adjust.
  3. Set up the starting position, choose your colour and level, then press Start game.
Top-down view of what the camera sees. If the gold grid lines up with the squares, calibration is good. Red tint shows squares that changed since the last confirmed position.

Game

Start the live camera (or take a photo) and mark the board corners. You can also play without a camera by tapping moves on the board below.
Tips for reliable detection
  • Mount the camera looking straight down (a phone stand, tripod or lamp arm). Steep angles let tall pieces hide neighbouring squares.
  • Use even light and avoid moving shadows. Keep the camera and board still for the whole game.
  • Live camera: after moving, take your hand away. The app reads the board once nothing has moved for about a second.
  • Photo mode: after each move, press Take photo. If the phone moved, drag the corner dots onto the board corners and press Re-read photo.
  • If a move is misread, tap a suggestion or tap the correct move on the screen board. Use Re-sync board when the real board matches the screen but the app is confused.
  • The engine’s move is spoken, shown in large text and highlighted in green on the screen and the camera view. Play it on your board and the app confirms it, or press ✓ I made its move.
