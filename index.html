About Shatranj

Shatranj is a free chess coach that works with your real chessboard. Put your phone above the board: the camera reads your moves, and the Stockfish chess engine replies on screen and out loud. After every game you get a game review: accuracy, blunders, mistakes and brilliant moves, key moments explained in plain words, and what to learn from the game.

  • Play against Stockfish 19 from about 1400 Elo up to full strength, or two players on one screen, with chess clocks.
  • Elo rating with titles: Candidate Master, FIDE Master, International Master and Grandmaster (in-app titles).
  • Courses: opening repertoire (Ruy Lopez, Italian, Sicilian Najdorf, Queen’s Gambit Declined, King’s Indian, Caro-Kann, London), middlegame strategy, tactics, endgames, and mate in 1, 2 and 3 puzzles.
  • Import and review your games from Lichess and chess.com, and find your recurring mistakes.

Works in Chrome on Android phones and on laptops. No sign-up. Your games stay on your device.

Camera

Press “Start live camera”, or take a photo of the board.
👁 camera · tap
  1. Put the phone/webcam directly above the board, with the whole board in view.
  2. Tap the 4 corners of the squares area (where the 64 squares end, not the wooden frame or border), in any order. The app finds a1 by itself from the pieces. A magnifier helps you place each dot; drag a dot later to adjust.
  3. Set up the starting position, choose your colour and level, then press Start game. The engine plays only on the screen, so you move just your own pieces. You can leave the engine’s pieces off your board.

Tips for reliable detection
  • Mount the camera looking straight down (a phone stand, tripod or lamp arm). Steep angles let tall pieces hide neighbouring squares.
  • Use even light and avoid moving shadows. Keep the camera and board still for the whole game.
  • Live camera: after moving, take your hand away. The app reads the board once nothing has moved for about a second.
  • Photo mode: after each move, press Take photo. If the phone moved, drag the corner dots onto the board corners and press Re-read photo.
  • If a move is misread, tap a suggestion or tap the correct move on the screen board. Use Re-sync board when the real board matches the screen but the app is confused.
  • The engine’s move is spoken, shown in large text and highlighted in green on the screen and the camera view. Play it on your board and the app confirms it, or press ✓ I made its move.
